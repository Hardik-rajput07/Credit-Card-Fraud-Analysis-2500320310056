* **Problem Statement**



Credit card fraud does not happen very often, but when it does it costs banks

and customers a lot of money. It is hard to find because the fraud transactions

are hidden among thousands of normal ones. Banks need simple rules to flag

suspicious transactions, and they also need to know how many real frauds the

rules catch and how many honest customers they flag by mistake. In this

project I study a credit card transaction dataset, build some simple fraud

rules, and check how well they work.



* **Objectives**

1\. Clean the data and write down every problem I fix, so no duplicates are left.

2\. Make at least 3 fraud rules: high amount, odd hours, and many transactions

&#x20;  in a short time.

3\. Compare my flagged transactions with the real fraud labels and report

&#x20;  precision and recall.

4\. Build a Power BI dashboard that shows the flagged transactions, with at

&#x20;  least 3 slicers.



* **Business Questions**

1\. What part of all transactions is fraud, and how much money is lost?

2\. At what hours and amounts does fraud happen the most?

3\. Which merchant categories and places have the most fraud?

4\. How many frauds does each rule catch, and how many of its flags are really

&#x20;  fraud?

5\. Which cards make many transactions very quickly, and are those more likely

&#x20;  to be fraud?
